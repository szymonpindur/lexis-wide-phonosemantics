# Additive structural-control comparison — policy frozen before new fits

2026-10-06. Authorized overnight remediation comparison; not preregistered and not automatically a replacement primary analysis. Earlier results informed the question, not the membership of individual outcome models. Equal language discovery counts are not a success criterion.

## Target and construction

Control for shared productive morphology, recorded lexical roots/components, and recurring boundary constructions that can persist in lexicalized/borrowed words. A synchronic parse and a construction indicator need not agree: neither is silently relabelled gold-standard morphology.

For BOTH languages retain every nonconstant unique binary pattern from the October 3 supplied-morphology + retained Morfessor design AND the October 5 AI design. Retain all aliases and source identities. Only exact identical carrier sets are collapsed; zero/one constants are documented. No rarity, semantic, coefficient or significance filter is applied to nuisance columns. Historical Polish audit indicators remain: discarding valid information to equalize counts is prohibited.

Apply the SAME additional boundary-candidate rule to both languages: derive an affix inventory from all explicitly hyphen-marked supplied morphemes and AI affixes in the existing annotation exports; add a presence indicator for matching that literal prefix/suffix on an orthographic word with a nonempty remainder. Interfixes (both ends hyphenated) and unmarked lexical tokens are NOT converted to arbitrary substring controls. Existing surface-audit prefix/suffix literals also enter the inventory. All inventory tokens, origin aliases, carrier counts and example wordforms are exported BEFORE any new fit. No minimum literal length or support cutoff is invented. A boundary match is a structural candidate, not proof of a morphological parse. Short endings may be nonspecific; attribution and overlap are reported rather than assuming their correctness. This does not claim complete coverage of internal patterns, allomorphy or etymology.

## Interpretation safeguard

Adjustment-induced attenuation or non-identification is not causal proof of a morphological origin, and more controls are not automatically safer. Report when distinct phonological information is no longer available. A narrower count ratio alone is not validation; equal counts are neither sought nor expected.
